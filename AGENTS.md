# Codex Guidance

This repo is documentation-first until the first prototype begins.

## Project Priorities

- Treat founders and builders as customers.
- Keep matching as the product core.
- Treat the paid 7-day trial as a trust mechanism after a match.
- Avoid unpaid speculative labor.
- Avoid vague AI judgment. Prefer visible readiness checks and evidence-based workflows.
- Avoid equity/legal agreement handling in the MVP.

## Engineering Defaults

- Keep changes small and directly tied to the stated task.
- Add tests when code begins to encode matching, pricing, milestone, or proof-of-work behavior.
- Prefer boring, easy-to-deploy technology for the first prototype.
- Do not add payment processing until the non-payment workflow is validated.

## Product Vocabulary

Use:

- builder
- founder
- trial sprint
- proof-of-work record
- founder seriousness
- builder proof

Avoid:

- cheap labor
- supply/demand language for users
- AI decides
- guaranteed co-founder match
