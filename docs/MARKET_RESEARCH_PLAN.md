# Market Research Plan

## Purpose

Avoid building for a dead market. The project must validate that founders and builders both care enough to participate, and that payment expectations can work.

## Core Hypotheses

### H1: Founders Need This

Founders will use the platform if it helps them find credible builders and complete a small project while building trust.

Signals to validate:

- founders already ask for technical help in communities
- founders have paid small contractors before
- founders struggle to assess technical talent
- founders prefer a trial before hiring or discussing co-founder terms

### H2: Builders Need This

Unemployed or underemployed builders will use the platform if it offers paid, scoped work and credible proof-of-work.

Signals to validate:

- builders dislike unpaid tests or speculative startup work
- builders want portfolio evidence while job searching
- builders are open to lower-than-normal paid trials if scope is fair
- builders value verified project records

### H3: Pricing Can Work

Founders and builders can agree on trial stipends when:

- the scope is small
- the platform recommends fair ranges
- builders set minimum acceptable rates
- founders understand they are paying less than normal hiring but more than free labor

### H4: Matching Quality Is the Real Wedge

Users will return only if matches feel better than LinkedIn, Discord groups, job boards, or generic co-founder communities.

## Interview Targets

Interview at least:

- 10 solo founders
- 10 unemployed or underemployed builders
- 5 early-stage startup operators or community organizers
- 3 people who have tried co-founder matching or contractor platforms

## Founder Interview Questions

- What are you building?
- What kind of help do you need most?
- How have you tried to find that help?
- What went wrong?
- Would you pay for a scoped 7-day trial?
- What price would feel fair?
- What would make you trust a builder?
- What would make you worry about paying?
- Would you prefer browsing profiles or receiving recommended matches?

## Builder Interview Questions

- What kind of work are you looking for right now?
- Have you done unpaid startup work or take-home tests?
- What felt unfair?
- What would make a 7-day trial worth your time?
- What minimum payment would you accept?
- What proof would you want after the project?
- What makes a founder look serious?
- Would verified project records help your job search or portfolio?

## Dead-Market Warning Signs

Stop or pivot if:

- founders will not pay meaningful trial stipends
- credible builders refuse the price range
- both sides only want free networking
- disputes dominate user concerns
- users say existing tools already solve the problem well
- users like the concept but would not act soon

## MVP Validation Experiments

### Experiment 1: Concierge Matching

Manually collect 10 founder needs and 20 builder profiles. Match them by hand and see whether any pair agrees to a paid trial.

Success signal:

- at least 2 paid or seriously negotiated trial attempts

### Experiment 2: Trial Brief Test

Ask founders to create a 7-day trial brief and show it to builders.

Success signal:

- builders say the brief is clear and fair
- founders are willing to revise scope to fit fair pricing

### Experiment 3: Proof-of-Work Value Test

Show builders a mock proof-of-work record.

Success signal:

- builders say they would use it in portfolio, LinkedIn, resume, or job applications

### Experiment 4: Pricing Test

Test recommended ranges and builder minimums across several trial types.

Success signal:

- enough overlap exists between founder budgets and builder minimums
