# Validation Sprint

## Goal

Gather enough real-world evidence to decide whether Founder Builder Match is a living market or an attractive but weak idea.

## Sprint Length

14 days

## Target Evidence

- 10 founder interviews
- 10 builder interviews
- 3 trial brief reviews
- 1 concierge match attempt
- 1 public validation report

## Success Criteria

The idea is worth continuing if:

- at least 6 of 10 founders say they have recently tried to find builder help
- at least 4 founders would consider a paid 7-day trial
- at least 6 of 10 builders reject unpaid speculative work and like proof-of-work records
- at least 4 builders would consider a paid trial if the minimum rate is respected
- at least 1 founder-builder pair is willing to discuss a real or simulated trial

## Kill or Pivot Criteria

Pause or pivot if:

- founders consistently refuse meaningful trial stipends
- builders see the platform as low-paid labor
- both sides prefer free networking only
- disputes, legal concerns, or payment complexity dominate every conversation
- people like the idea but would not act within 30 days

## Founder Interview Recruiting

Target:

- solo founders
- non-technical or semi-technical founders
- founders with a prototype or validated problem
- founders who recently tried hiring, finding a co-founder, or finding a contractor

Places to recruit:

- local startup communities
- LinkedIn founder posts
- university entrepreneur groups
- founder Discord or Slack communities
- personal network

## Builder Interview Recruiting

Target:

- unemployed engineers
- underemployed engineers
- designers or product builders seeking portfolio work
- builders open to startup work
- builders who dislike unpaid take-home tests

Places to recruit:

- LinkedIn layoff/job-search posts
- GitHub or portfolio communities
- developer Discord groups
- local tech meetups
- personal network

## Concierge Match Experiment

Before building more software, run one match manually:

1. Collect one founder need.
2. Collect three builder profiles.
3. Score them using founder need, builder proof, founder seriousness, budget overlap, and timezone.
4. Show the top match and reasons to both sides.
5. Ask whether they would enter a paid or simulated 7-day trial.
6. Record objections and pricing feedback.
