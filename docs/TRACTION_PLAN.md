# 72-Hour Traction Plan

## Goal

Collect enough early signal to improve the project and make a future Codex for Open Source application more credible.

This is not a growth campaign. It is a validation sprint with visible public artifacts.

## Target Outcomes

Within 72 hours:

- 5 founder-side feedback notes
- 5 builder-side feedback notes
- 3 hosted demo reviews
- 1 simulated concierge match
- 1 validation report draft

## Day 1: Direct Outreach

Send 15 personal messages:

- 5 founders or people who have hired builders
- 5 builders or people looking for work
- 5 community operators, startup friends, or technical friends

Use:

- [docs/OUTREACH_PACK.md](OUTREACH_PACK.md)
- [docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md)
- hosted demo: https://bruceclng-cloud.github.io/founder-builder-match/

Public tracking:

- founder notes -> issue #6
- builder notes -> issue #10
- prototype feedback -> issue #8

## Day 2: Concierge Match Simulation

Pick one scenario:

- full-stack tutor MVP
- AI operations proof-of-concept
- design-led waitlist

Ask one founder-side person and one builder-side person:

- Is this match believable?
- Is the price fair?
- Is the trial scope fair?
- What would stop you from proceeding?

Public tracking:

- concierge notes -> issue #9
- dead-market signals -> issue #7

## Day 3: Report And Adjust

Write a short validation report:

- strongest positive signal
- strongest negative signal
- most confusing part of the demo
- pricing feedback
- whether to continue, narrow, pivot, or pause

Use:

- [docs/VALIDATION_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md](VALIDATION_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md)

## Minimum Useful Evidence

The project becomes meaningfully stronger if it can show:

- at least one person on each side understood the product without a long explanation
- at least one builder says the proof-of-work record would help
- at least one founder says a paid 7-day trial could reduce risk
- at least one person gives a hard objection that changes the roadmap

## What Not To Do

- Do not spam public groups.
- Do not ask "Would you use this?" as the main question.
- Do not hide negative feedback.
- Do not claim real traction from polite compliments.
- Do not present simulated matches as real user activity.
