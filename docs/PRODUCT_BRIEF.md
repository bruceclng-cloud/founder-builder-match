# Product Brief

## Working Name

Founder Builder Match

## One-Liner

A fair matching platform for founders and unemployed or underemployed builders, with paid 7-day trial sprints to test real collaboration.

## Problem

Founders often need technical, product, design, or operational help before they are ready to hire full time. Builders who are unemployed, underemployed, or looking for meaningful startup work often need paid experience, proof-of-work, and credible opportunities.

The current market has several gaps:

- founders struggle to judge builder quality
- builders are asked to do unpaid speculative work
- co-founder matching is vague and trust-poor
- job boards do not fit pre-company or early startup collaboration
- generic networking produces too much noise

## Target Customers

The platform serves both sides as customers.

### Founder Customer

Founders need:

- credible builders for specific skill gaps
- a way to test collaboration before committing
- progress on a small project
- lower cost than normal hiring
- structure around scope, payment, and expectations

### Builder Customer

Builders need:

- paid trial work
- access to serious founder opportunities
- proof-of-work records
- portfolio evidence
- protection from unpaid speculative labor
- a path into startup experience or longer-term collaboration

## Core Product Clarification

Matching is the heart of the platform. The 7-day trial project is the trust mechanism after a promising match.

The product should not drift into a generic project-posting tool. Its core promise is:

> Find a serious founder or credible builder, then test fit through a fair paid trial.

## Differentiators

### 1. Matching Based on Practical Signals

Initial matching should use:

- founder need
- builder skill proof
- founder seriousness

### 2. Paid Trial Sprints

The trial sprint makes the match actionable. A founder pays a small but fair stipend, and the builder completes a scoped 7-day deliverable.

### 3. Proof-of-Work for Builders

Builders should receive verified project records showing:

- role
- scope
- deliverables
- skills used
- artifacts
- completion status
- founder feedback when available

### 4. Staged Readiness Checks

The product should not feel like a black-box AI check. It should guide users through visible checks:

- founder clarity
- budget
- timeline
- market evidence
- skill need
- builder proof
- availability
- trial expectations

## Initial Business Model

Founder pays the trial stipend. The platform takes 10%.

Pricing uses a hybrid model:

- platform recommends a fair range based on project type and scope
- builder sets a minimum acceptable trial rate
- founder budget must overlap with the builder minimum before a match can proceed

## Risks

The main risks are:

- builders may feel trial fees are too low
- disputes may become messy
- users may complete one trial and not return
- legal/payment/equity issues may become complicated
- founders may be unserious
- matching quality may be hard before enough users exist

## MVP Boundary

The MVP should avoid:

- equity handling
- co-founder legal agreements
- complex arbitration
- broad social networking
- trying to serve every founder and every builder from day one

The MVP should focus on:

- matching serious founders with credible builders
- creating fair paid trial workflows
- generating proof-of-work records
