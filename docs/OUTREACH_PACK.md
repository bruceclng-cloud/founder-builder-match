# Outreach Pack

Use these messages to get real feedback without over-pitching.

## Founder / Hiring Side

```text
Hey [Name], I’m testing an open-source prototype around matching people hiring for early projects with credible builders through fair paid 7-day trial sprints.

Not selling anything. I’m trying to see if the problem is real.

Could you look at this demo and answer 2 questions?

Demo: https://bruceclng-cloud.github.io/founder-builder-match/

1. If you were hiring for an early project, what part would feel useful or trustworthy?
2. What would make you say no?

If easier, just reply here with rough thoughts.
```

## Public Post Draft

```text
I’m testing a small open-source prototype:

Founder Builder Match: matching people hiring for early projects with builders looking for fair paid trial work.

The wedge:
- not unpaid speculative work
- not a generic job board
- not a co-founder promise
- a scoped paid 7-day trial + proof-of-work record

Demo:
https://bruceclng-cloud.github.io/founder-builder-match/

I’m looking for blunt feedback from both sides:
1. If you hire builders, would this reduce trust/risk?
2. If you are a builder, would this feel fair?
3. What would make this fail?
```
