# Interview Guide

## How To Use

Keep interviews short. The goal is not to pitch. The goal is to learn whether the pain, payment, trust, and proof-of-work assumptions are real.

Do not ask leading questions like "Would this app be useful?" Ask about recent behavior first.

## Founder Interview

### Screening

- What are you building?
- What stage is it at?
- Have you tried to find technical, design, product, or growth help in the last 6 months?

### Problem

- What kind of help did you need?
- Where did you look?
- What made it hard?
- Did you pay anyone?
- Did you consider a co-founder, contractor, employee, or advisor?

### Trust

- How did you judge whether someone was credible?
- What would make you trust a builder enough to start?
- What would make you reject a builder?

### Trial Sprint

- Would a scoped 7-day paid trial help you decide whether to continue?
- What project would you realistically scope for 7 days?
- What would count as done?
- What budget range would feel fair?
- What would make the trial feel risky?

### Closing

- Would you want to review a prototype?
- Would you consider a simulated or real trial with a matched builder?

## Builder Interview

### Screening

- What kind of work are you looking for now?
- Are you unemployed, underemployed, freelance, employed but available, or exploring?
- Have you done unpaid tests, trial projects, or speculative startup work?

### Problem

- What makes founder/startup opportunities attractive or unattractive?
- What makes you think a founder is serious?
- What information do you need before talking to a founder?

### Payment

- What is the lowest fair 7-day trial stipend for a small scoped project?
- Does a lower rate become acceptable if the scope is small and proof-of-work is verified?
- What would make pricing feel exploitative?

### Proof-of-Work

- Would a verified trial record help your portfolio, job search, or LinkedIn?
- What should a proof-of-work record include?
- What should stay private?

### Closing

- Would you want to review a prototype?
- Would you consider a simulated or real trial with a matched founder?
