# Contributing

Founder Builder Match is early. Contributions should keep the project focused on fair founder-builder matching and paid trial sprints.

## Good First Contributions

- improve product docs
- add market research notes
- design intake forms
- prototype screens
- model matching criteria
- write edge cases for trial sprint fairness
- improve contributor onboarding

## Product Principles

- Both founders and builders are customers.
- Builders should not be asked to do unpaid speculative labor.
- Founders should get clear, practical matching and scoped progress.
- Milestones should be judged by evidence and pre-agreed criteria.
- The MVP should avoid equity agreements and complex legal workflows.

## Development

There is no app scaffold yet. When development starts, add setup commands and verification steps here.

## Issues

When opening an issue, include:

- the user side affected: founder, builder, maintainer, or platform
- the workflow affected
- why it matters
- a proposed solution or open question
